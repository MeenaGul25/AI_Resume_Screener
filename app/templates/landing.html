Powerful Features

Everything HR Needs for Smarter Screening

The system combines document processing, natural language processing, semantic similarity, skill analysis, and candidate evaluation into one recruitment workflow.

Resume & JD Upload

Upload resumes and job descriptions in PDF or DOCX format and automatically extract their textual content.

Semantic Matching

AI-based semantic analysis compares the meaning of resume and job-description content rather than relying only on exact keywords.

Skill Extraction

Identify relevant technical and professional skills and compare them against the requirements of the selected job.

Candidate Scoring

Combine multiple matching factors into a final candidate score and classification for HR review.

Experience & Education

Analyze candidate experience and education against the requirements defined in the job description.

Plagiarism Detection

Compare uploaded resumes to identify highly similar documents and provide a plagiarism or similarity assessment.

Simple Workflow

From Resume to AI-Powered Decision Support

A simple four-step workflow designed for HR professionals.

01

Upload

Upload the candidate resume and the relevant job description.

02

Extract

The system extracts and processes important textual information from both documents.

03

Analyze

AI evaluates semantic similarity, skills, experience, education, and document similarity.

04

Match

HR receives a final score, classification, recommendation, strengths, and weaknesses.

Intelligent Analysis

More Than Keyword Matching

  • Sentence-BERT semantic similarity
  • ESCO-based skill identification
  • Experience and education evaluation
  • Resume similarity and plagiarism analysis
  • HR recommendation and candidate classification
